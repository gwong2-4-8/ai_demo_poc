# Tables

* [Properties Master Table](properties.md) - Parcel-level structural attributes, hazard scores, in-place lease terms, and Cloud Storage inspection image URIs.
* [Maintenance Requests](maintenance_requests.md) - Unstructured tenant and field-inspector maintenance tickets partitioned by request date.
* [Regional Rent History](regional_rent_history.md) - Monthly metro and property-type average market rent time series for TimesFM forecasting.
* [Property Valuation Comps](property_valuation_comps.md) - Verified comparable property leases and physical attributes for TabFM zero-shot rent valuation.
