# Metrics

* [Net Effective Rent Yield](net_effective_rent_yield.md) - Annualized net rental income after operating expenses and unmitigated roof/structural hazard reserves divided by estimated asset value.
* [Severe Hazard Exposure Rate](severe_hazard_exposure_rate.md) - Share of active portfolio units flagged with urgent multimodal inspection hazards or open habitability emergencies.
